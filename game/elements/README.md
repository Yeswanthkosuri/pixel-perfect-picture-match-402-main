# Elemental System — 12 Powers & Reactions

Elemental Arena features twelve distinct elemental affinities. Each character equips two elements: one **Primary Resonance** (governs core abilities, visuals, and passive perks) and one **Secondary Resonance** (governs utility, reactions, and combination surges).

## Catalog of 12 Elements

| Element | Theme | Primary Offensive Role | Utility / Defense Role | Signature Ability |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fire** | Combustion & Heat | High burst AoE & burning DoT | Smoke concealment, thermal push | *Inferno Orb* — explosive fireball |
| **Lightning** | Electric Plasma | High-velocity chain bolts, interrupt | EMP disruption, energy overdrive | *Thunder Surge* — chain arc strike |
| **Water** | Hydrodynamics | Pressure knockback, dousing | Healing surges, ground slick | *Tidal Torrent* — repelling wave |
| **Earth** | Terramancy | Heavy impact, physical projectiles | Stone barrier creation, fortification | *Bouldercrush* — crushing rock pillar |
| **Wind** | Aerodynamics | Projectile deflection, razor gales | Extended gliding, speed drafts | *Gale Dash* — aerial repositioning |
| **Ice** | Cryomancy | Slowing chills, freeze shatter | Permafrost traps, defensive ice block | *Frost Spike* — impaling freeze dart |
| **Light** | Photonic Energy | Piercing continuous laser lances | Blind flashes, stealth reveal | *Prism Beam* — focused laser |
| **Darkness** | Shadow & Void | Life leech, fear suppression | Shadow cloaking, darkness dome | *Void Tether* — draining shadow link |
| **Nature** | Flora & Bio-toxin | Poison stacks, thorn barriers | Regeneration blooms, root snares | *Verdant Entangle* — root trap |
| **Gravity** | Spacetime Pull | Singularity wells, gravitational slam | High-jump wells, repulsion field | *Graviton Vortex* — pull field |
| **Cosmic** | Astral Energy | Micro-meteor volleys | Teleportation blinks, stasis | *Astral Rift* — phased blink strike |
| **Sound** | Resonance & Sonic | Armor-shattering shockwaves | Sonic disorientation, echo-location | *Resonance Pulse* — cone wave |

## Elemental Combinations & Reactions
When two elements interact on a target or the environment:
- **Fire + Water = Scalding Steam** (Blinds vision and deals continuous ambient damage).
- **Water + Lightning = Electrified Conduction** (Stuns all targets within wet puddle).
- **Fire + Earth = Molten Lava** (Leaves hazard zones on the colosseum floor).
- **Ice + Sound = Cryo Shatter** (Deals 150% critical damage to frozen enemies).
- **Wind + Fire = Conflagration** (Spreads fire area over double radius).
