# Special Abilities System — 5-Slot Loadout Architecture

Each character loadout in Elemental Arena equips **five** active/passive special abilities in addition to their two elemental affinities and primary weapon.

## 5-Slot Loadout Structure
- **Slot 1 (Mobility / Traversal)**: Dash, Grappling Hook, Wall Climb, Double Jump.
- **Slot 2 (Primary Elemental Power)**: Bound to Primary Element (e.g., Inferno Orb).
- **Slot 3 (Secondary Elemental Power / Defense)**: Bound to Secondary Element or Barrier (e.g., Temporary Shield).
- **Slot 4 (Tactical / Support)**: Tactical Scan, Regeneration, Quick Reload, Super Strength.
- **Slot 5 (Ultimate / Surge)**: Charged high-impact ability (e.g., Elemental Singularity).

## Complete Catalog of 12 Special Abilities
1. **Super Sprint** (Mobility | Active | CD: 8s): 3-second burst of 180% movement speed.
2. **Double Jump** (Mobility | Passive): Allows a mid-air secondary leap to reach high ledges.
3. **Air Dash** (Mobility | Active | CD: 6s): Directional horizontal air thrust in current movement direction.
4. **Grappling Hook** (Mobility | Active | CD: 10s): Fires tether pulling character towards ledges or structures.
5. **Super Strength** (Offense | Toggle): Increases melee and knockback force by 75% at the cost of stamina drain.
6. **Reinforced Armor** (Defense | Passive): Flat 15% damage reduction to all incoming physical impacts.
7. **Regeneration** (Support | Passive): Restores 5 HP/sec when out of combat for 4 seconds.
8. **Temporary Shield** (Defense | Active | CD: 18s): Projects an impenetrable 75-point absorption barrier for 4s.
9. **Shadow Cloak** (Defense | Active | CD: 22s): 5-second stealth cloak; broken upon attacking.
10. **Tactical Scan** (Support | Active | CD: 15s): Highlights enemy positions through walls for 4s within 40m.
11. **Quick Reload** (Support | Active | CD: 5s): Instantly replenishes weapon ammunition/heat gauge.
12. **Wall Climb** (Mobility | Toggle | CD: 12s): Enables scaling vertical arena surfaces for up to 6 seconds.
